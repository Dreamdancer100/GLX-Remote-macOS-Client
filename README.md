## ✨ Features

- 🚀 **Help on your Mac:** Give your GLX ID to your helper and solve problems together on your screen.
- 🧩 **Free, no licence:** Costs nothing and needs no key. It only receives help and cannot control other devices.
- ⚙️ **Guided setup:** A tour leads you through the screen recording and accessibility permissions, with ticks when done.
- 🔒 **Guide button per permission:** Extra step-by-step help next to every permission; permissions stay after updates.
- 📂 **Screen, mouse and keyboard:** Your helper sees your screen and can operate it.
- ⚡ **Chat:** Write with your helper during the session.
- 🎨 **Files in both directions:** Send and receive files during the session.
- 🌍 **Sound and microphone:** Talk to your helper directly.
- 🛠️ **Always in control:** You see when a session is running and end it with one click.
- 📊 **End-to-end encrypted:** ECDH and AES-256; native app for Intel and Apple silicon, 12 designs, German and English.

## 🧩 Requirements

- macOS 13 Ventura or newer
- Intel Mac or Apple silicon (M1 and newer)
- Internet connection

## 🔐 Privacy

Every connection is end-to-end encrypted (ECDH and AES-256). Nobody can connect without you, and the relay server cannot read anything.
